Google Cloud · Secure Video Access for Hospitals
The challenge

Doctors need procedure videos. Sharing them must be secure, approved and time-limited.

Doctors at your partner hospitals regularly ask for recordings of procedures. The files are large and contain patient information. Every share needs to be safe, compliant, and easy for a busy clinician to use.

🩺

Who needs the videos

Doctors and clinical staff at partner hospitals. They don't have accounts with you and shouldn't need a Google account.

📦

What they're downloading

Procedure recordings, often hundreds of gigabytes and sometimes more than a terabyte.

🧑‍💼

Who decides

Your team receives each request, checks it, and approves or declines it.

Our recommendation

SFTPGo on Google Cloud

It meets every requirement today: data stays in your Google Cloud, password + MFA sign-in, approval and expiry built in, and a simple browser experience for doctors, with a robust option for very large files. It's already built and tested as a working proof of concept.

The trade-off: a small amount of infrastructure for your team. Google-managed Kubernetes and a managed database keep that light.

Strong alternative

Serverless portal

If running no infrastructure at all is essential, this is the Google-native alternative. Doctors sign in with password + MFA and download directly from Cloud Storage using short-lived links. There are no servers to run.

The trade-off: very large files are best downloaded with a download manager rather than a plain browser.

How it works

Built entirely on Google Cloud

Two separate front doors: one for doctors, one for your team. Behind them, everything runs inside your Google Cloud project and is recorded.

Why it meets HIPAA
  • One BAA: every service that touches the videos is a Google Cloud service under your BAA
  • Strong sign-in: password + authenticator code for doctors, company sign-in + MFA for your team
  • Encrypted: in transit, and at rest with a separate key per hospital
  • Minimum access: each doctor sees only their approved files, read-only
  • Automatic expiry: sign-in stops and copies are deleted when the window ends
  • Audit trail: every request, approval, sign-in and download is kept for 6 years
Why SFTPGo
  • Supports password + MFA for people outside your organization
  • Simple browser downloads for doctors, plus SFTP for very large files, which resumes if interrupted
  • Reads directly from Cloud Storage, with no file servers or extra copies
  • Locks each account to one folder, the hospital's network, and an end date
  • Driven automatically by the review console, so nothing is set up by hand
  • Open source, with an Enterprise edition for commercial support

Security controls at a glance
  • Accounts start switched off; files are copied only at approval, into a folder for that request alone
  • Doctors can only connect from approved hospital networks
  • The review console is never exposed publicly and talks to the download service over a private network
  • No stored keys in code; services use short-lived Google credentials
  • Revoke in one click, or switch off a hospital's key to cut off its files instantly
